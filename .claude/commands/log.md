description: A call, a decision, a corrective action, onto the record the moment it happens. Against a lot, run, case, order, settlement, fisher, room or customer.
---

Run `npm run seafood -- log <record> "what happened"`. The record can be a lot (`LND-2105`), a run, a case, an order, a settlement, a fisher's name, a room name or a customer. Write what was said or decided and who decided it, in one or two sentences. Never paraphrase a lab result or a temperature; quote it.
